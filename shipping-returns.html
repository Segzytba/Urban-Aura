    Shipping & Returns

    Everything you need to know about getting your order and what to do if something's not right.

    Shipping & Delivery

    We ship anywhere in Nigeria and worldwide. Because delivery cost and delivery time depend on your location, we don't charge or estimate shipping automatically at checkout — instead, once you place an order, we'll confirm the exact delivery cost and expected delivery time with you directly, via WhatsApp or email.

    • Within Nigeria: we'll reach out shortly after your order to confirm delivery cost and timing for your state.
    • International orders: we'll confirm shipping cost and delivery time with you directly before your order ships, since this varies a lot by country.

    You'll get an order confirmation by email as soon as payment goes through. From there, we keep you updated on your order's status via WhatsApp or email — if you haven't heard from us within 48 hours of ordering, feel free to message us on WhatsApp with your order reference.

    Returns & Exchanges

    If something doesn't fit right, you can request an exchange within 7 days of delivery.

    • Exchanges only — we offer a size or color swap of equal value. We don't offer cash refunds.
    • Condition: items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags still attached, in their original packaging.
    • Return shipping: the cost of sending the item back to us is covered by you, the customer.
    • How to start an exchange: message us on WhatsApp with your order reference and what you'd like to exchange for — we'll walk you through the rest.

    Defective or Wrong Item

    If you received a defective item or the wrong item due to our error, that's on us — message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll send a free replacement at no cost to you, including shipping.

    This policy applies to orders placed through Urban Aura's official website. Have a question not covered here? Reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll help directly.
