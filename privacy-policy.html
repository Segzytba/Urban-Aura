    Privacy Policy

    This policy explains what personal information Urban Aura collects, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it — including under Nigerian data protection law.

    1. Introduction

    Urban Aura ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy applies to everyone who visits urban-aura.netlify.app or places an order with us, whether you're based in Nigeria or elsewhere.

    We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA), Nigeria's principal data protection law, enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). Where applicable, we also aim to respect the data protection expectations of customers ordering from outside Nigeria.

    2. Information We Collect Directly From You

    We collect information you actively provide, specifically:

    • At checkout: full name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and country/state or city.
    • If you message us: whatever you share with us via WhatsApp or our on-site chat widget (e.g. sizing questions, order issues).
    • Your cart: the items, sizes, and quantities you add to your cart, held in your browser until you check out.

    We do not collect or store your card or bank details at any point — payment is handled entirely by Paystack, our payment processor.

    3. Information Collected Automatically

    Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers (Netlify and Supabase) automatically log standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This is used only for security, fraud prevention, and keeping the site running reliably; we don't use it to build advertising profiles.

    4. How We Use Your Information

    • To process, confirm, and fulfill your order.
    • To contact you about delivery cost, delivery timing, or any issue with your order.
    • To send you an order confirmation email.
    • To respond to questions or support requests you send us.
    • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of the site.
    • To meet legal or regulatory obligations (e.g. tax or consumer protection record-keeping).

    We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

    5. How We Disclose Your Information

    We only share personal data with third parties where it's genuinely necessary to run the store, or where the law requires it:

    • Paystack — processes your payment. We never receive or store your card details ourselves.
    • Supabase — our database provider, which stores your order details securely.
    • EmailJS — sends your order confirmation email.
    • Netlify — hosts the website itself.
    • Law enforcement or regulators — if we're legally required to disclose information (e.g. by court order, or to the NDPC), or to protect our legal rights.

    We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

    6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

    We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We do use your browser's local storage to remember your cart between visits, and temporary session storage to make the shop page load faster on repeat visits within the same browsing session. Both stay on your own device and are cleared when you clear your browser data.

    7. Data Security

    We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the entire site and restricted, access-controlled storage for order data. No method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure, but we take reasonable, appropriate steps to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

    8. Retention of Your Information

    We keep order records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the order, handle any related exchange or dispute, and meet our legal, accounting, or tax obligations. If you ask us to delete your personal data and we have no legal obligation to keep it, we will do so.

    9. Your Rights

    Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

    • Be informed about how your data is collected and used (this policy).
    • Access the personal data we hold about you.
    • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Request erasure of your data, where we have no legal obligation to keep it.
    • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
    • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
    • Data portability — request your data in a portable format, where applicable.

    To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference or the email/phone number you used to check out. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

    10. International Users

    We ship worldwide, so we may process and store the personal data of customers outside Nigeria (including via Supabase's and Netlify's infrastructure, which may be located outside Nigeria). By ordering from outside Nigeria, you understand your information may be processed in a different country than your own. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is protected to a comparable standard regardless of where it's processed.

    11. Children's Privacy

    Urban Aura is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us via WhatsApp and we will delete it.

    12. Third-Party Websites & Links

    Our site links to third-party platforms we don't control — including Instagram, WhatsApp, and Paystack's payment page. This policy doesn't cover how those platforms handle your data; we encourage you to review their own privacy policies before sharing information with them.

    13. Complaints

    If you believe we've mishandled your personal data, please reach out to us first via WhatsApp so we can try to resolve it directly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the regulatory body responsible for enforcing the NDPA.

    14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this policy from time to time as the site or our practices change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date below. We encourage you to check back periodically.

    Last updated: August 2026. This policy is provided in good faith to be clear and transparent about our data practices, and does not constitute legal advice. Questions about this policy, or about your data? Reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll help directly.
